INTENTIONAL DIFFERENCES REQUIRED BY THE BRIEF
- Original supporting copy, fixtures, and demonstration actions.
- Independent monochrome/ui wordmark and explicit attribution.
- Working mobile previews instead of the upstream static dashboard image.
- No colored chart/status/destructive tokens; no gradients.
- Lucide icons instead of the repository's mixed icon-family dependencies.
- A complete dashboard and feature sections below the main preview, as requested.
- Themes/Templates routes from the brief rather than claiming to reproduce
  the upstream Directory/Typeset products or its full documentation corpus.
- Financial controls, chat, QR, and profile actions are honest local demos.
- A readable chart data alternative, stronger control/focus boundaries, and
  corrected numeric progress semantics.
- Neutral OKLCH roles and 0.625rem radius follow the final supplied theme.
  Dark/light chart and destructive roles remain achromatic. Placeholder text
  and keyboard-key labels use a stronger neutral on muted surfaces; explicit
  focus outlines use a high-contrast neutral rather than a translucent ring.
- Geist Sans, cv11/ss01 font features, and -0.011em body tracking follow the
  final typography steering. Existing heading-specific tracking is retained.
- components.json selects radix-nova for future CLI additions. The official
  homepage's preview composition itself mixes style families; the setup preset
  alone does not reproduce that composition.
